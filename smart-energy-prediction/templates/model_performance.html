Metric Explanations
R² (Coefficient of Determination): Measures how well predictions match actual values. 1.0 = perfect, 0 = no correlation.
MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average absolute difference between predicted and actual values. Lower is better.
RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Similar to MAE but penalizes larger errors more heavily. Lower is better.
