How It Works

Predictions powered by
physics + machine learning

From raw electrical parameters to actionable insights — in seconds.

1
Enter Parameters
Input voltage (V), current (A), usage hours, appliances, temperature, and previous consumption.
2
Normalize with Scaler
StandardScaler normalizes all inputs to zero mean and unit variance for unbiased ML inference.
3
Random Forest Predicts
100 decision trees ensemble-vote to produce a precise kWh prediction with R²=0.984 accuracy.
4
Personalized Insights
Get consumption level (LOW/NORMAL/HIGH), bill estimate, carbon footprint, and saving tips.
